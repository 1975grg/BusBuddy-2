Questions to Ask Replit About Security

You can send something like this directly to Replit support / sales:

A. Infrastructure & Compliance

Where is data physically hosted?

“Which cloud provider and regions does Replit use to host code and data?”

What security certifications or audits do you have?

“Do you have SOC 2, ISO 27001, or other third-party security audits?”

Do you have a documented security whitepaper or security overview you can share?

B. Data Protection (Encryption, Backups, Retention)

Is all data encrypted in transit and at rest?

“Are HTTPS/TLS used for all connections? Is stored data encrypted at rest?”

How are backups handled?

“Do you back up project data? How long are backups retained and how is access to them controlled?”

Can you describe your data retention and deletion policies?

“If we delete a project or data, how long before it is fully removed from your systems and backups?”

C. Access Control & Isolation

How is my app isolated from other customers’ apps?

“What isolation mechanisms are used so that other Replit users can’t access our code or data?”

Who at Replit can access our projects or databases, and under what conditions?

“Is access logged and limited to specific staff roles (e.g., for support only)?”

Are there tools or settings to restrict team member access (e.g., role-based permissions)?

D. Network & Application Security

Do you provide protections like firewalls, DDoS mitigation, and rate limiting for deployed apps?

How do you handle vulnerabilities?

“Do you run vulnerability scans on your infrastructure? What is your process and typical response time for patching critical issues?”

Do you have a bug bounty or responsible disclosure program for security researchers?

E. Privacy & Student Data (very important for schools)

How do you handle personal data for applications that may involve minors?

“Do you have any guidance or features that help apps built on Replit comply with FERPA / COPPA or similar student-privacy requirements?”

Do you act as a data processor for our app data, and can you provide a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) if needed by the school district?

Who are your sub-processors (other vendors) who may have access to our data, and where are they located?

F. Incident Response

What is your incident response process?

“If there is a security breach that might affect our app, how and how quickly would we be notified?”

Do you provide any uptime / availability guarantees (SLA) for production deployments?
